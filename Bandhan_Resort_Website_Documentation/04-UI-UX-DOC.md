# UI/UX Design Document: Bandhan Resort Website

## 1. Primary user tasks

1.  Understand what Bandhan Resort offers.
2.  View real photos of the property.
3.  Check the location and directions.
4.  Call or message the resort.
5.  Ask about a stay, event, banquet, or other confirmed service.

## 2. Navigation

Recommended desktop links: - Home - About - Gallery - Facilities -
Events (if confirmed) - Contact

Header CTA: "Enquire Now".

On mobile, use a compact menu and keep a visible Call button where
practical.

## 3. Homepage flow

1.  Header
2.  Photo-led hero
3.  Short introduction
4.  Gallery preview
5.  Confirmed facilities
6.  Events or stay information, depending on confirmed services
7.  Location and directions
8.  Enquiry CTA
9.  Footer

## 4. Contact UX

-   Make the phone number tap-to-call.
-   WhatsApp CTA must use the owner-confirmed number.
-   Map CTA should open the supplied Google Maps place.
-   The enquiry form should be short and usable on mobile.
-   Clearly state how the enquiry is delivered.
-   Do not claim "booking confirmed" unless there is an actual booking
    workflow.

## 5. Gallery UX

-   Use thumbnails with consistent ratios.
-   Open images in an accessible lightbox or link to a full image.
-   Provide close button, Escape-key support, keyboard focus handling
    and alt text.
-   Avoid loading all full-resolution photos immediately.
-   Do not show duplicate or unrelated photos.

## 6. Trust and content

-   Show accurate location and contact details.
-   Only show reviews/ratings with approval and attribution.
-   Avoid fake testimonials, fake room prices, fake capacities, or
    unsupported facility badges.
-   If services are not confirmed, omit them rather than presenting them
    as fact.

## 7. Accessibility

-   Keyboard-operable menu and gallery.
-   Clear focus indicators.
-   Correct labels and error messages.
-   Adequate contrast.
-   Reduced-motion support.
-   Descriptive image alt text.

## 8. Usability QA

Test on: - 320px - 375px - 390px - 414px - 768px - 1024px - 1280px -
1440px

Check call links, WhatsApp link, map directions, navigation, gallery,
form validation and footer links.
