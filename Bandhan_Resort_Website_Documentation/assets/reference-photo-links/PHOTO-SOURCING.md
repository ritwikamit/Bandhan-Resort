## What the public gallery indicates

Justdial reports 46 photos in total, including 16 exterior photos, 33
owner-uploaded photos, 1 user-uploaded photo, and 4 360-view photos.
Categories may overlap; do not add category counts together as a
unique-photo count.

Gallery descriptions include a banquet hall with stage decor, an
entrance/reception area, an exterior building with a lawn, and a paved
courtyard. These descriptions came from the public gallery page, not
from a local copy of the original image files.

## Why original image files are not included

The page's underlying photo files could not be reliably fetched into
this package during preparation. This ZIP therefore does not include
copied or fabricated resort images. The website developer should
retrieve photos from the resort owner or use the public pages to
identify images, then obtain permission and direct source files before
adding them to the production website.

## Recommended workflow

1.  Ask the owner for 10--20 high-resolution originals, preferably via
    Drive/WhatsApp.
2.  Request separate images for exterior, lawn, rooms (if any),
    banquet/event spaces, dining (if any), and entrance.
3.  Confirm that images represent the current property and current
    facilities.
4.  Record source and permission.
5.  Resize/crop and export as WebP/AVIF.
6.  Place approved files under `public/images/`.
7.  Use descriptive filenames and meaningful alt text.
8.  Do not hotlink Justdial images or use images from Facebook without
    permission and a stable approved source.
