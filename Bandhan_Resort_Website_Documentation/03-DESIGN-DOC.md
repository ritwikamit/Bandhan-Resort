# Design Document: Bandhan Resort Website

## 1. Design direction

Build a warm, welcoming hospitality website that feels genuine and
local, with clear photography and simple navigation. The website should
communicate the venue's outdoor spaces and event potential without
exaggerating facilities that have not been confirmed.

## 2. Visual principles

-   Photography-led design.
-   Generous whitespace and readable type.
-   Simple navigation and clear contact actions.
-   Use natural greens and warm neutrals as a starting direction, then
    align to the resort's actual logo/signage once supplied.
-   Avoid generic luxury clichés, excessive gradients, glassmorphism,
    and cluttered animations.
-   Use real resort imagery rather than stock photos wherever possible.

## 3. Provisional palette

Use as a proposal only, pending owner approval: - Forest green:
`#254D3A` - Leaf green: `#66845B` - Warm ivory: `#F7F4EC` - Sand:
`#D9C9A8` - Charcoal: `#252824` - White: `#FFFFFF`

## 4. Typography

-   Headings: a readable display serif or restrained modern sans-serif.
-   Body: a highly legible sans-serif.
-   Avoid loading multiple font families without need.
-   Ensure Hindi/Devanagari support if Hindi content is included.

## 5. Hero

-   Full-width owner-approved exterior or lawn image.
-   Clear headline and one sentence describing confirmed services.
-   Primary CTA: "Call to Enquire" or "Plan Your Visit".
-   Secondary CTA: "View Gallery" or "Get Directions".
-   Use a subtle overlay only to maintain text contrast; avoid heavy
    dark filters that hide the property.

## 6. Gallery

-   Mix exterior, lawn, event hall/stage, rooms, and dining photos only
    when those images and services are confirmed.
-   Use a clean grid with a lightbox if it improves usability.
-   Do not infer all images depict current facilities; verify that
    images are current and belong to the resort.
-   Provide captions only where the content is confirmed.

## 7. Motion

-   Subtle fade/reveal on entry.
-   Gentle image hover scale on desktop.
-   Smooth menu transitions.
-   Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
-   Avoid heavy parallax or video backgrounds that slow mobile users.

## 8. Responsive behavior

-   Mobile navigation collapses cleanly.
-   Gallery uses one/two columns on small screens and wider grids on
    desktop.
-   Contact actions should be easy to tap.
-   Buttons should have comfortable touch targets.
-   Avoid horizontal overflow.

## 9. Brand assets

No official logo file was supplied with this request. Use a tasteful
text wordmark during development, then replace it with the owner's
approved logo. Do not invent an official logo or use third-party logos.
